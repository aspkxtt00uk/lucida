Layered bouquet — 22 flower templates

Every flower from your photo, cut out one by one, all at one shared scale so the pieces fit back together exactly. The small gypsophila is left out on purpose for you to dot in by hand.

All templates use the same scale: 30 photo pixels = 1 cm. The finished bouquet is 27.3 × 35.7 cm (fits on A3). Each sheet has a 5 cm bar — in Lucida, pinch the overlay until that bar matches a real ruler on your paper, and the flower will be exactly the right size. To make the whole bouquet bigger or smaller, scale every piece by the same amount.

How to build it

  1. Base sheet (#0). Draw the wrapping paper and leaves at 27.3 × 35.7 cm. The white dashes show where each flower will sit.
  2. Each flower, 1 → 22. Tap Trace lines in Lucida, match the 5 cm bar to a ruler, trace onto paper, then colour it using the colour sheet as reference.
  3. Cut on the solid black line — including the hatched flap. The hatched part is hidden in the finished piece: it slides under the flower(s) named on the sheet, so no gaps show.
  4. Glue in number order, 1 (back) to 22 (front), using the assembly map. For a real 3D effect, lift the front pieces on foam pads — thicker pads for higher numbers (the lily #22 and gerberas #19 and #21 sit highest).
  5. Gypsophila last. Dot it on top of everything at the end — under it, every flower is complete.
